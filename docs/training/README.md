# Training decks

PowerPoint decks for the internal training library (LMS). Each content slide has an annotated screenshot whose numbered callouts match the numbered steps beside it, and the speaker notes hold the narration.

| Deck | Audience |
| --- | --- |
| Otta training 1 - The website | Otta team |
| Otta training 2 - The admin portal | Otta team |
| Otta training 3 - The broker portal | Otta team |
| Otta Broker Portal guide | Brokers, sent when they join |

The broker guide is also published as a PDF at `public/documents/guides/otta-broker-portal-guide.pdf`. It is linked from the welcome email (`onboarding.emails.invite` in `src/content/onboarding.ts`) and from the portal's Help & contact page. If the portal changes, re-export the PDF from the guide deck.

Screenshots were taken from the demo data, so every name, policy and broker in them is fictional.
